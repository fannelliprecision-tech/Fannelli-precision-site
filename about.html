About the company

Built on precision. Owned by the work.

Fanelli Precision is owner-operated — every project gets direct oversight from the person whose name is on it.

Fanelli Precision was founded on a simple premise: most residential contracting in this area doesn't hold itself to a real standard. Jobs get rushed, communication gets vague, and homeowners are left guessing what happens next.

We built the opposite of that — fixed-price estimates, materials locked before demo, hidden conditions handled as transparent change orders instead of silent scope creep, and a response time on new inquiries measured in minutes, not days.

Every project — from a single tile job to a full bathroom remodel — runs through the same operating discipline. That consistency is the actual product, not just the finish work.
