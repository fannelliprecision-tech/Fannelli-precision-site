About the company

Built on precision. Backed by real accountability.

Fannelli Precision is a general contracting company built to hold itself to a higher standard than what's typical in this industry.

Fannelli Precision was founded on a simple premise: too much residential and commercial contracting in this area doesn't hold itself to a real standard. Jobs get rushed. Communication gets vague. Homeowners are left guessing what happens next, and why the price changed halfway through.

We built the opposite of that. Every project starts with a detailed written estimate. Every change is discussed and confirmed before it happens, not discovered on the final invoice. Every inquiry gets a real response, usually the same day, from a person who's actually going to be involved in the work.

That consistency, not just the finish work but the discipline behind it, is the actual product. It's what separates a contractor you hire once from one you trust with the next project, and the one after that.

The person behind the work

Meet the owner.

William Angeles, Founder of Fannelli Precision

Fannelli Precision is owned and run directly by William Angeles, who built the company around a straightforward idea: contracting should be run like a real business, not a side hustle. Systems, accountability, and a standard that doesn't slip once the deposit clears.

William brings a background in multiple languages and deep, authentic ties across Central Pennsylvania's communities, allowing Fannelli Precision to communicate clearly and build trust with clients who aren't always well-served by contractors who only speak English. That access isn't a marketing angle; it's a real advantage built from real relationships.

Every project that comes through Fannelli Precision is shaped by the same standard William holds for his own name: do the work right, communicate honestly, and never leave a client guessing.
